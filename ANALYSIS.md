# Tiny Sales Analysis

## Project goal

This beginner-friendly R project examines sales for five products. It shows how to import a CSV file, create a new variable, calculate summary statistics, and make a basic chart.

## Method

Revenue was calculated with this formula:

`revenue = units sold × price`

The R script then calculated total revenue, average revenue per product, and the product with the highest revenue.

## Results

- Total revenue: **$590**
- Average revenue per product: **$118**
- Best-performing product: **Backpack**, with **$200** in revenue
- Pen generated the lowest revenue: **$60**

## Conclusion

Backpacks were the strongest product in this small dataset. Although fewer backpacks were sold than pens, their higher price produced more revenue. This shows why both sales volume and product price matter when evaluating performance.
